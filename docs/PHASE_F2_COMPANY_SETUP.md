# Phase F2 Company Setup And Role Acceptance

Last updated: July 24, 2026

## Purpose

F2 turns Stonegate's existing operating controls into a repeatable employee and partner setup
process. It does not create a second user, role, market, compensation, script, capacity, buyer, or
team system.

## What Was Added

- Nine standard operating seats for the initial Stonegate structure.
- Primary and backup coverage for each seat.
- Planned, hiring, covered, and paused seat states.
- Verified closing attorney, title company, funding partner, inspector, and other counterparties.
- Company readiness checks that read existing compensation, market, launch, script, closer, and
  buyer records.
- Versioned role-manual assignments.
- Employee workspace-test submissions.
- Owner approval, return-for-changes, and revocation decisions.
- Audit events for every setup change and decision.

## Owner Workflow

1. Open **Operating Model**.
2. Select **Company setup**.
3. Select **Install standard setup** once.
4. Set each seat to Planned, Hiring, Covered, or Paused.
5. For a covered seat, select the primary person and optional backup.
6. Add each closing or operating partner, then verify it after checking the relationship.
7. Assign the correct role manual to each employee.
8. Review submitted workspace-test evidence.
9. Approve it, return it for changes, or revoke an old approval.

The system rejects inactive users and users who do not hold the role required by the seat or
manual. Owners can temporarily cover any seat.

## Employee Workflow

1. Sign in with your individual account.
2. Open **My Setup**.
3. Review the assigned role and manual version.
4. Test the pages and actions used in normal work.
5. Confirm that unrelated or restricted areas are unavailable.
6. Describe what was tested and submit it.
7. Address manager feedback if the test is returned.

An employee cannot approve their own test or open the owner setup controls.

## Readiness Meaning

The readiness panel is an operating checklist, not a launch guarantee. A completed item means the
required Stonegate record exists and is active or approved. Legal review, provider acceptance, and
real-world testing remain separate finishing-roadmap requirements.

## Acceptance Checklist

- Install is idempotent and does not duplicate seats.
- Covered seats require an active, correctly authorized user.
- Staff can view and submit only their own role acceptance.
- Only operating-model managers can assign or decide acceptances.
- Closing partners remain pending until a manager verifies them.
- Deactivated partners no longer satisfy closing-partner readiness.
- Every create, update, submission, approval, and revocation creates an audit event.
- The owner overview shows current readiness from existing operational records.
