# Stonegate Staff Role Manuals

Last updated: July 24, 2026

## How To Use This Guide

Every employee uses their own Stonegate login. Open **My Setup** to see the role manual assigned to
you. Test your normal workspace, record what you tested, and submit it to your manager.

The rule for every role is simple: use Stonegate as the company record, document the next action,
and do not work around a blocked action or missing approval.

## Owner

Main pages: Dashboard, Operating Model, Operations, Approvals, Finance, Marketing, and AI Control.

- Set policy, staffing, market, compensation, and approval authority.
- Cover open operational seats without hiding which job was performed.
- Approve role setup only after testing the employee's normal access and restrictions.
- Review exceptions and approvals; do not silently change historical evidence.
- Deactivate departing users immediately and reassign their open work.

## Lead Manager

Main pages: Lead Desk, Inbox, Work Queue, Calendar, and Seller Pipeline.

- Accept warm leads, qualify the seller, and schedule appropriate appointments.
- Keep an owner, next action, and due date on every active lead.
- Do not promise price, change an approved offer, or bypass communication restrictions.
- Use `LEAD_MANAGER_USER_MANUAL.md` as the detailed daily guide.

## VA Caller

Main page: Prospecting.

- Work only assigned calling batches and the approved script.
- Record an accurate disposition after every attempt.
- Create a warm handoff only when the seller shows genuine interest.
- Preserve opt-outs, wrong numbers, and do-not-contact requests immediately.
- Do not export lists or access underwriting, buyers, contracts, finance, or unrelated leads.

## Acquisitions Closer

Main pages: Calendar, Field Operations, Underwriting, Inbox, and the assigned Lead Record.

- Review the meeting brief and unresolved questions before the appointment.
- Confirm condition, occupancy, decision-makers, access, and material property facts.
- Negotiate only within approved authority.
- Record the inspection, seller concerns, outcome, and next action.
- Do not alter approved ARV, offer ceilings, or contract terms without the required approval.

## Transaction Coordinator

Main pages: Transactions, Approvals, Inbox, and Calendar.

- Own deadlines and evidence from signed seller contract through funded closing.
- Track earnest money, title, payoff, due diligence, assignments, and closing requirements.
- Keep documents tied to the correct deal and source.
- Escalate missing or conflicting evidence instead of marking a gate complete.
- Do not make legal decisions or fabricate provider status.

## Dispositions

Main pages: Dispositions, Buyers, Transactions, Inbox, and Calendar.

- Prepare approved deal packages and match only appropriate buyers.
- Track inquiries, showings, offers, proof of funds, deposits, primary buyer, and backup buyer.
- Keep restricted economics inside authorized roles and approved audiences.
- Record opt-outs and buyer qualification changes.
- Do not select a buyer or release a package by bypassing approval gates.

## Finance And Accounting

Main page: Finance.

- Record source-linked revenue, deductions, marketing costs, compensation, and payouts.
- Reconcile funded deals before commissions are marked payable.
- Keep company margin, role compensation, and owner distributions separate.
- Correct errors through traceable adjustments, not deleted history.
- Do not approve operational offers, contracts, or buyer selection through Finance access.

## Marketing

Main pages: Marketing, Campaigns, and approved aggregate reporting.

- Create trackable campaigns and keep source, cost, and conversion evidence current.
- Use approved audiences, consent rules, suppression controls, and company senders.
- Review cost per lead, appointment, contract, and funded deal.
- Do not expose seller details in unnecessary exports or AI prompts.
- Do not treat a modeled attribution result as confirmed revenue.
